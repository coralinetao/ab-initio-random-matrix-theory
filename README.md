# Ab Initio Random Matrix Theory of Molecular Electronic Structure

This repository contains the data and analysis scripts associated with:

**Zhen Tao and Victor Galitski,  
"Ab Initio Random Matrix Theory of Molecular Electronic Structure"**

This repository provides the electronic-structure data and analysis scripts used
to generate the figures in the main text and Supplemental Material.

## Repository structure

The repository is organized primarily by figure. Each figure directory contains
the data and scripts required to reproduce the corresponding analysis.

- `Fig1/` – Nearest-neighbor level-spacing statistics of HF unfolded
  single-particle orbital energies for methyloxirane, alanine,
  1-phenylethylamine, benzene, and symmetry-broken benzene.

- `Fig2/` – Representative real-space and momentum-space wavefunctions
  of high-energy orbitals of symmetry-broken benzene.

- `Fig3/` – Nearest-neighbor level-spacing statistics of CIS excitation 
   energies (interacting) and orbital energy differences (non-interacting) 
   for methyloxirane, alanine, 1-phenylethylamine, benzene, and symmetry-broken benzene.

- `Fig4/` – Spectral form factors of the molecular many-particle spectra.

- `Fig5/` – Nearest-neighbor level-spacing statistics in an external
  magnetic field and the GOE-to-GUE crossover.

- `Fig6/` – Parametric evolution of electronic energy levels under an
  external electric field and nuclear motion.

- `Fig7/` – Level-velocity and level-curvature statistics of
  symmetry-broken benzene in an external electric field.

- `Fig8/` – Nearest-neighbor level-spacing statistics of bound and
  full electronic spectra of symmetry-broken helicene chains.

- `FigS4/` – Average gap ratio statistics along the interpolated symmetry-breaking path of benzene.

- `geometries/` –  Molecular coordinates.
